What are the key performance indicators (KPI) you will use to evaluate your design ideas? 

The following will we use to evaluate our design idea.                                                                 
  1. Task completion rate
    •	The percentage of help requests posted that result in a successfully closed exchanges.
    •	This is the single most indicator to see our the platform is delivering real value. 
    •	A high posting rate with a low completion rate indicates a mismatch between supply and demand or trust barriers.

  2. Time-to-first-response
    •	how quickly a requester receives a chat message after posting 
    •	If requesters wait hours for a response, the platform loses its real-time, peer-to-peer feel.

  3. Supplier return rate
    •	The percentage of users who complete a second supply exchange within next few weeks
    •	This metric tells us whether the experience of giving other help in our system is rewarding enough to bring the users back.

  4.  Score and review from users:
    •	The score show how the users engage in the app daily and weekly
    •	The review and rating from one user to another users also reflect how it work for the idea in the app.

  5. Feedback system:
    •   We also have the feedback system which allow users to give us some feedback to refine our app

	

If you have encountered significant risks, requiring critical changes to the original plan, explain the risks, the management process, and details of the modifications made.
Risks & Changes Made                                                                                
  1.  Safety & Uncertainty in Real-world Exchange Tasks
    •    Even with reminders and consent forms, we could not fully control or predict what might happen during real-life help/exchange interactions between users.
    •    If it has an emergency problem, ask for the professional help first. 
    •    Added clearer guidelines and boundaries for interactions and exchange

  2.  User Verification Constraints
    •   Our original plan required verification using student accounts + phone numbers, but:
    •   when we try to implemnet in the app, we have No access to university authentication systems
    •   And SMS verification requires cost and external services
    •   To management: we may more rely on log in system, and link to google account to double check the user email.
The above changes allowed us to reduce risk while still achieving our core project goals.
